A wealthy business owner hosted a party at their cottage.
During the party, a locker hidden behind a bookshelf in the
Study Room was robbed. Eight people were present and one of
them is the CULPRIT. The owner's PA has hired you as a
private detective to investigate the case.

YOUR MISSION: visit the 7 locations, interview the 8 people,
place every letter on the Scrabble board, form both words,
and accuse the culprit before your credibility runs out.

Good luck, Detective.
